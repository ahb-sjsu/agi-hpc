\textbf{Opener (30 s).} Most AI safety is one number at the output. We keep the structure:
who's affected, what's owed, who consented, who carries the risk. We collapse to a decision
only at the end, and record how. Every decision is sealed in a hash-chained proof you can
replay. Open source, on PyPI, running today.\\[2pt]
\textbf{Then ask:} ``Model side or agent side?''\quad Model $\to$ \textbf{6}.\quad Agent $\to$ \textbf{5} then \textbf{7}.\quad
\textbf{10 s version:} a safety layer that seals every decision in a replayable proof, and compares what a model says with what its activations show.}}

\pn{3 Pluralism} Four lenses: consequences, duties (Z3), virtue, care. Never averaged. When they

\textbf{0.155} ($\approx$20k letters). Warns at 0.25, blocks at 0.30.

\pn{5 Gateway} Planner $\to$ Reflex / Tactical (10--100\,ms) / Strategic $\to$ allow, revise, block.

\pn{6 PyTorch lens} Forward hooks: activation lens vs.\ text lens $\to$ requires-human-review.

\hd{Questions}
\textbf{Whose ethics?} It isn't claimed to be right. Four lenses, no averaging, a human decides
conflicts. The claim is auditability.

\textbf{Real robot?} No. Simulated 3D home, scripted proposals, no hardware. The agent stack and
the two NPCs are live.

\textbf{Speed?} Tactical 10--100\,ms, reflex faster.

\textbf{Service crashes?} Rule fallback. Fails closed.

\textbf{Lens works?} Early. Mid-layer result replicated 2 of 2. Final layer fails validation.
Not a finished detector.

\textbf{Why group theory?} It's a symmetry test you can run. We claim only $V_4$. Corrected $D_4$
ourselves. Earlier gauge model falsified by CHSH tests on human judgments ($N{=}600$, all $|S|\le 2$).

\textbf{COMPAS?} LBI \textbf{1.049} [1.027, 1.072], $z=4.9$. $\approx$1.00--1.02 under
matching parity. A screening signal, not a regulatory audit.

\textbf{Install?} \texttt{pip install erisml-compiler} (alpha 0.9.0, MIT). erisml-lib 3.1.0 on
PyPI. The worked example needs the source tree.

\textbf{Not done?} Ranks 1--3 real, higher partial, action axis a stub. Hohfeld/Bond Index in
erisml-lib, not the compiler yet. $D_4$ posited.

\textbf{PyTorch engineer extra:} turboquant-pro on PyPI. One-line HF KV cache drop-in, Triton
kernels, vLLM plugin, $\approx$5$\times$ less KV memory.
